Data Analyst Remote Jobs in Philippines
A short summary for Data Jobs in the Philippine Job Market

About Dataset
PH Data Analyst Market Overview
-comprises 51 records detailing current job opportunities within the Philippine data analytics sector. It tracks five primary variables: Job Title, Company, Location, Salary Range (Monthly), and Work Arrangement.

Overview
This dataset contains a collection of job listings for Data Analyst roles, primarily sourced to facilitate research into the modern job market, skill requirements, and salary trends. It provides a granular look at what employers are looking for, including technical requirements, company profiles, and compensation ranges.

Context
With the rapid evolution of the data industry, staying informed about regional demands and skill-gaps is critical for both job seekers and educators. This dataset was compiled to provide a "snapshot" of the industry, allowing for deep-dives into Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks, salary prediction, and geographic analysis.

Potential Use Cases
-Skill Extraction (NLP): Use the Job Description column to identify the most in-demand tools (SQL, Python, Tableau) using TF-IDF or Word Embeddings.

-Salary Prediction: Build a regression model to predict the median salary based on Location, Industry, and Company Size.

-Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Visualize which cities or sectors currently have the highest density of data-related openings.

-Sentiment Analysis: Analyze the tone of job descriptions across different industries.
