About Dataset
Techsalerator’s Real Estate & Airbnb Data for the Philippines

Techsalerator’s Real Estate & Airbnb Data for the Philippines delivers a comprehensive and data-driven view of the country’s residential property landscape and short-term rental market. This dataset is designed to support investors, developers, researchers, travel platforms, and policy analysts seeking insights into property values, rental trends, occupancy performance, and market dynamics across the Philippines’ major metropolitan areas and tourism-driven destinations.

Top 5 Key Data Fields
Property Location (City, Province, Latitude & Longitude)
Identifies precise geographic placement of residential and short-term rental properties.

Property Type
Categorizes listings by condominium, house, apartment, vacation rental, shared space, or commercial-use properties.

Listing Price & Estimated Value
Captures sale prices, long-term rental rates, Airbnb nightly prices, and estimated market valuations.

Occupancy & Availability Rate
Tracks how frequently Airbnb and short-term rental properties are booked across seasons and regions.

Host & Property Performance Metrics
Includes reviews, ratings, booking frequency, cancellation rates, and host responsiveness.

Top 5 Real Estate & Airbnb Market Trends in the Philippines
High Demand in Urban & Tourism Hubs
Metro Manila, Cebu, Boracay, and Palawan lead in both real estate development and Airbnb activity.

Growth of Condominium-Based Rentals
Condominiums dominate short-term rental listings, especially in city centers and business districts.

Tourism-Driven Short-Term Rental Demand
Beach destinations and island provinces experience strong seasonal occupancy rates.

Rising Interest from Foreign & Local Investors
Increased investment in vacation properties and rental units across key regions.

Expansion of Flexible & Mid-Term Rentals
Growing demand from digital nomads, remote workers, and returning overseas Filipinos.
